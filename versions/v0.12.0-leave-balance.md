# v0.12.0 — Leave balance projection

- Saves annual and sick leave balances from the user's latest LES.
- Supports user-entered annual and sick accrual rates per pay period.
- Deducts future annual and sick leave detected in schedule imports.
- Deducts approved future leave from My Leave imports and avoids double-counting matching dates.
- Projects annual and sick balances through the end of the federal leave year.
- Warns about projected negative balances and annual leave above the carryover limit.
- Uses editable 240-hour carryover, 4-hour sick accrual, and 4/6/8-hour annual accrual defaults.
